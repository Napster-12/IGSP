Applicability: All suppliers and prospective suppliers registering for or using the Icebolethu Group Supplier Portal (IGSP)

1.1. Where the supplier is a company, close corporation, partnership or other legal entity, the individual accepting these Terms and Conditions on its behalf confirms that they are authorised to act for and bind that entity.

1.2. The Portal will record the applicable version of the Terms and Conditions and the date and time of acceptance. Suppliers are responsible for retaining a copy of the applicable terms for their records.

1.3. Icebolethu Group may amend these Terms and Conditions when reasonably necessary to reflect changes in its procurement requirements, business processes, applicable law or Portal functionality. Suppliers will be notified of material changes and may be required to accept the revised terms before continuing to use the Portal.

2. Definitions

For purposes of these Terms and Conditions:

2.1. “Icebolethu Group” means Icebolethu Group and the relevant business entities within the Group that use its procurement processes, subject to the applicable legal arrangements.

2.2. “Supplier” means any individual, company, close corporation, partnership or other entity applying for registration or registered on the Portal for the purpose of supplying goods, services or rentals to Icebolethu Group.

2.3. “Procurement” means the Icebolethu Group function responsible for supplier onboarding, sourcing, supplier management and procurement governance.

2.4. “Compliance documents” means documents and information requested by Icebolethu Group for supplier registration, due diligence and ongoing compliance, including company registration records, director or owner identification, tax information, B-BBEE documentation, banking confirmation, proof of address and declarations.

2.5. “Supplier registration certificate” means the certificate issued through the Portal following approval of a supplier's onboarding application.

2.6. “Active supplier” means a supplier whose Portal account is active and whose registration has not been suspended, deactivated or otherwise restricted.

2.7. “Personal information” has the meaning assigned to it under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”).

3. Purpose and scope of the Portal

3.1. The Portal provides an electronic platform through which suppliers may:

  1. Create and manage a supplier account;
  2. Complete and submit supplier onboarding applications;
  3. Upload and update compliance documents;
  4. View application statuses, notifications and review comments;
  5. Access and maintain a supplier registration certificate, where applicable;
  6. Update eligible supplier information and renew relevant certificates; and
  7. Maintain product and service catalogue information when the supplier is approved and active.

3.2. The Portal supports supplier registration and procurement administration. It does not, by itself, constitute a tender, quotation request, purchase order, contract award, appointment or commitment by Icebolethu Group to procure any goods or services.

3.3. The Portal does not process invoices, supplier payments or purchase orders. Suppliers must follow the applicable Icebolethu Group procedures and instructions for any procurement or commercial transactions conducted outside the Portal.

3.4. Access to or use of the Portal does not create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency relationship or exclusive supplier arrangement between the supplier and Icebolethu Group.

4. Supplier onboarding and documentation

4.1. Suppliers must complete all applicable sections of the onboarding application and provide the information and documents required for their business type and selected supplier category.

4.2. The required documentation may include, where applicable:

  1. Company registration documentation issued by CIPC (Companies and Intellectual Property Commission);
  2. Identification documents for directors, members, shareholders or owners, as applicable;
  3. Valid SARS tax and VAT documentation or details required for supplier's tax status verification;
  4. A valid SANAS approved B-BBEE certificate or Sworn B-BBEE Affidavit;
  5. A business bank confirmation letter;
  6. Proof of business address;
  7. Completed declarations and other prescribed forms; and
  8. Additional supporting documentation reasonably required for supplier verification or due diligence.

4.3. Bank confirmation letters and proof of address must be dated within the preceding three months at the time of submission, unless Procurement confirms an alternative requirement in writing.

4.4. Suppliers must provide valid issue and expiry dates for SARS VAT and B-BBEE certificates where those dates are applicable and required by the Portal.

4.5. Compliance documents must be legible, complete, authentic, current and relevant to the supplier applying for registration. Documents must be uploaded in the file format and within the size limits prescribed by the Portal.

4.6. Suppliers must not submit forged, altered, misleading, fraudulent or materially incomplete documents, or knowingly provide information that misrepresents the supplier's legal status, ownership, tax status, B-BBEE status, banking details or ability to supply goods and services.

4.7. Icebolethu Group may request additional information, clarification, replacement documents or updated declarations where necessary to complete its review.

4.8. The submission of documents through the Portal does not mean that those documents have been independently verified or accepted by Icebolethu Group. Suppliers acknowledge that external verification with CIPC, SARS, B-BBEE verification bodies, banks or other relevant parties may not be automated and that document review may be performed manually.

4.9. Suppliers are responsible for ensuring that information and supporting documents remain accurate and current throughout their registration and any subsequent supplier relationship.

4.10. Where a supplier's ownership, directors, shareholders, legal name, registration status, tax status, banking details or other material particulars change, the supplier must notify Procurement and provide updated supporting documentation where required.

5. Director, shareholder and ownership disclosures

5.1. Suppliers must accurately disclose the directors, members, shareholders, owners and other particulars requested during onboarding.

5.2. Suppliers must ensure that the individuals whose personal information is submitted have been appropriately informed and that the supplier has the necessary authority or lawful basis to provide that information to Icebolethu Group for supplier registration and due diligence.

5.3. The Portal may identify a director, shareholder or owner whose details are already linked to another supplier application or supplier record.

5.4. Where such a match is flagged, the application will be subject to Icebolethu Group's applicable supplier registration rules and review process. Under the current Portal rules, an application flagged for a duplicate director, shareholder or owner may only be declined.

5.5. Suppliers must not conceal ownership relationships, submit misleading particulars or create additional accounts to circumvent supplier registration controls.

5.6. Suppliers acknowledge that the submission of accurate ownership and director information is necessary for supplier identification, governance, conflict-of-interest assessment and audit purposes.

6. Tax, B-BBEE and ongoing compliance

6.1. Suppliers must maintain the tax, VAT, B-BBEE and other compliance status applicable to their business and to the goods or services they intend to provide.

6.2. Suppliers must upload renewed SARS VAT and B-BBEE certificates or other required updated documents before the current documents expire.

6.3. The Portal may issue reminders approximately 30 days and seven days before the recorded expiry dates of applicable VAT and B-BBEE certificates.

6.4. Suppliers acknowledge that the Portal is configured to deactivate an approved supplier's account when a monitored certificate expires. An expired certificate may therefore result in the supplier losing active Portal status and access to supplier catalogue functionality.

6.5. The Portal is also configured to reactivate an account following the upload of a renewed certificate. Suppliers acknowledge that an upload or automated status change does not remove their responsibility to ensure that the replacement document is authentic, valid and applicable to their business. Icebolethu Group may take further action if the document is found to be invalid or the supplier otherwise fails to meet applicable requirements.

6.6. Suppliers must respond promptly to compliance-related requests and must not knowingly continue to represent themselves as fully compliant where required documentation has expired, been withdrawn or become invalid.

6.7. Suppliers must inform Procurement if any submitted compliance information becomes materially inaccurate or if a relevant certificate is cancelled, suspended, withdrawn or otherwise ceases to be valid.

7. Supplier catalogue and product or service information

7.1. Suppliers whose accounts are active may be permitted to maintain catalogue entries for the goods, services or rentals they offer, subject to the Portal's functionality and Icebolethu Group's applicable requirements.

7.2. Suppliers must ensure that all catalogue information is accurate and up to date, including, where applicable:

  1. Product or service descriptions and categories;
  2. Prices and applicable units of measure;
  3. Stock quantities or availability;
  4. Lead times and delivery-related information; and
  5. Supplier details and the relevant province or area of operation.

7.3. Suppliers must promptly correct or remove information that is inaccurate, outdated, unavailable or no longer applicable.

7.4. Catalogue entries must not contain misleading claims, unauthorised use of third-party intellectual property, unlawful content or information that misrepresents the supplier's goods or services.

7.5. Catalogue information is provided to assist Icebolethu Group personnel in identifying and comparing potential suppliers. A catalogue listing, displayed price, stock quantity or lead time does not constitute an offer accepted by Icebolethu Group or a commitment to purchase.

7.6. The Portal's catalogue functionality does not constitute a purchase order, formal request for quotation, tender award or contractual agreement. Any purchase must be undertaken through the applicable Icebolethu Group procurement and approval processes.

7.7. Suppliers must not assume that inclusion in the catalogue guarantees visibility to every relevant employee, inclusion in a sourcing exercise, selection for business or any minimum level of expenditure.

7.8. Suppliers must disclose any existing or previous business relationship with Icebolethu Group that is relevant to supplier registration, procurement governance, conflict-of-interest assessment or the allocation of procurement business. This includes existing supplier arrangements with any Icebolethu Group business unit and any material personal, financial or business relationship with an Icebolethu Group employee or representative involved in procurement decisions, where such disclosure is required by applicable policy or reasonably necessary to assess a potential conflict of interest.

7.9. Suppliers must provide complete and accurate information in response to questions concerning these relationships and must promptly disclose any material change in the information previously provided.

7.10. The existence of a disclosed business relationship does not, in itself, disqualify a supplier from registration or consideration for procurement opportunities. Icebolethu Group may assess the nature of the relationship and implement appropriate measures to manage any identified conflict of interest.

7.11. A supplier's deliberate concealment of a material business relationship, provision of false or misleading information, or failure to disclose a relationship where disclosure was expressly required may result in the application being declined, the supplier's registration being suspended or deactivated, or the termination of an existing contractual relationship, where justified by the circumstances and permitted by the applicable contract and law.

7.12. Before taking action based on an alleged failure to disclose, Icebolethu Group may seek clarification and consider the materiality of the omission, the circumstances in which it occurred, whether it was intentional, and any applicable contractual or legal requirements.

8. Procurement governance and ethical conduct

8.1. Suppliers must conduct themselves honestly, professionally and ethically in all dealings with Icebolethu Group and its employees, representatives and business units.

8.2. Suppliers must comply with applicable laws and regulations, as well as relevant Icebolethu Group procurement policies, supplier requirements, codes of conduct and contractual obligations communicated to them.

8.3. Suppliers must not offer, promise, give, request or accept bribes, kickbacks, improper inducements or other benefits intended to influence a procurement decision or obtain an improper business advantage.

8.4. Suppliers must not attempt to improperly influence the evaluation, approval, allocation or award of procurement business, including through personal relationships, undisclosed interests, gifts or other benefits.

8.5. Suppliers must disclose actual, potential or perceived conflicts of interest relevant to their dealings with Icebolethu Group, including relevant relationships with all employees, decision-makers or other parties involved in procurement, where disclosure is required by law or applicable Icebolethu Group policies.

8.6. Suppliers must cooperate with reasonable due diligence, compliance reviews, audit enquiries and requests for information relating to their registration or supplier relationship.

8.7. Suppliers must not submit false declarations, conceal material information or use another entity's documents, credentials or registration details to obtain approval.

8.8. A breach of this section may result in an application being declined, registration being suspended or deactivated, or other action permitted by applicable law, policy or contract.

9. Notices and communication

9.1. Suppliers must maintain a valid email address and current contact details in their Portal accounts.

9.2. Icebolethu Group may communicate application statuses, review comments, compliance reminders, account changes and other supplier-related notices through the Portal and/or the registered email address.

9.3. Suppliers are responsible for checking their Portal accounts and registered email addresses regularly, including spam or junk folders, and for responding to requests within any stated timeframes.

9.4. A supplier's failure to monitor its notifications does not remove its obligation to maintain accurate information or comply with applicable requirements.

9.5. General supplier registration enquiries may be directed to the Icebolethu Group call centre on 031 208 9102. Any additional official support or Procurement contact details will be communicated through the Portal or Icebolethu Group's designated channels.

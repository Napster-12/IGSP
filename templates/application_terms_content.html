These Supplier Application Terms and Conditions ("Application Terms") apply when you submit a supplier application through the Icebolethu Group Supplier Portal (the "Portal"), and for as long as you remain a registered supplier. They add to the Portal Terms and Conditions you accepted when you created your account. Where the two conflict on a matter relating to your application or supplier registration, these Application Terms apply. Words defined in the Portal Terms have the same meaning here.

1. Authority to apply

1.1. By submitting an application you confirm that you are authorised to apply on behalf of the business named in the application, and to accept these Application Terms on its behalf.

1.2. Each Portal account can hold one supplier application. Submitting an application does not make you an approved supplier, and does not guarantee any order, contract or volume of business.

2. Accuracy of information

2.1. You warrant that everything in your application is true, complete and current, including your company details, address, contact details, supplier categories, and the details of every director, shareholder and owner.

2.2. You must keep your information up to date through the Portal. If your details change after your application has been approved or placed on file and the Portal no longer lets you edit them, you must tell us without delay using the contact details in clause 12.

2.3. Information that is false, misleading or incomplete, or documents that are altered or forged, will lead to your application being declined or your supplier account being deactivated, and may be reported to the relevant authorities.

3. Required documents

3.1. Your application must include all of the following documents. The Portal will not let you submit until each one has been uploaded:

  • CIPC company registration document.
  • Certified ID copies of all directors.
  • SARS VAT certificate.
  • Confirmation of bank account letter, not older than 3 months.
  • A valid B-BBEE certificate, a letter from your accountant, or a sworn affidavit.
  • Proof of the company's address, not older than 3 months.
  • The signed Declaration of Interest form.

3.2. You may also upload a company profile and other supporting documents.

3.3. Documents must be uploaded as PDF files. Each document must be legible, complete and belong to the business applying. The bank account named in your confirmation letter must be in the name of the business applying.

4. Certificates with expiry dates

4.1. When you upload your SARS VAT certificate and your B-BBEE certificate, accountant's letter or affidavit, you must enter its correct issue date and expiry date.

4.2. The Portal monitors these dates. We will remind you by email and in the Portal 30 days and 7 days before a certificate expires.

4.3. If a certificate expires before you upload a renewed one, your supplier account will be deactivated automatically, and your products will no longer appear in our catalogue. Your account will be reactivated automatically once you have uploaded renewed certificates and none of your certificates has expired.

4.4. It is your responsibility to keep your certificates valid. We are not liable for any loss of business while your account is deactivated because a certificate expired.

5. Directors, shareholders and owners

5.1. A director, shareholder or owner should be part of only one supplier on the Portal. The Portal checks the identity numbers in your application against other suppliers. If a person in your application is already linked to another supplier, your application will still be submitted, but it will be flagged to our procurement team and declined.

5.2. You must list every director, shareholder and owner correctly. Leaving people out of your application or entering their details incorrectly is a breach of these Application Terms.

5.3. You must declare any relationship between your business, or any of its directors, shareholders or owners, and Icebolethu Group or any of its directors or employees, in the Declaration of Interest form. You must tell us promptly if such a relationship arises later.

6. How we review your application

6.1. After you submit, your application is reviewed by our procurement team. We may verify your information and documents with third parties, as set out in the Portal Terms.

6.2. You can follow the status of your application in the Portal. We will also notify you by email when it changes. The outcomes are:

  • Returned for update: we need you to correct or add information or documents. You can edit the application and resubmit it.
  • Approved: your supplier account is activated and a supplier registration certificate is issued.
  • On file: your information and documents are in order, but we do not need your services at present. We will keep your details and contact you when we do.
  • Declined: your application was not successful. You may edit and resubmit it if your circumstances change.

6.3. All decisions are at Icebolethu Group's sole discretion. We are not obliged to give reasons for a decision, or to approve an application that meets the minimum requirements.

7. Supplier registration certificate

7.1. On approval, the Portal issues a supplier registration certificate. It is valid for 12 months from the date of approval, unless your account is deactivated sooner.

7.2. The certificate confirms your registration on our supplier database only. It is not a contract, purchase order or guarantee of business, and you may not use it to represent that you are an agent of, or endorsed by, Icebolethu Group.

8. Inventory and catalogue

8.1. Once your account is active, you may list the products and services you offer, with their prices, units, availability and lead times.

8.2. Listed items are shown to Icebolethu Group's procurement staff in our internal catalogue, together with your business name, contact details and location, so that they can compare suppliers and contact you.

8.3. You must keep your listings accurate and current. Prices must be in South African Rand and must state clearly whether VAT is included where this is relevant. Remove or hide items you can no longer supply.

8.4. Orders are not placed through the Portal. Our staff will contact you directly. Only a written purchase order or Supplier Agreement from Icebolethu Group binds us. A listing in the catalogue does not.

9. Deactivation of your account

9.1. Besides the automatic deactivation in clause 4.3, we may deactivate your supplier account at any time if you breach these Application Terms or the Portal Terms, if your information or documents are found to be incorrect, or if we no longer require your services.

9.2. While your account is inactive you cannot use your inventory, and your products are hidden from our catalogue.

10. Records of your application

10.1. The Portal keeps a record of your application, including each change made to it, the date and time of the change, and the IP address it was made from. It also records the version of these Application Terms you accepted and when you accepted them.

10.2. We may use these records to manage your registration, resolve disputes and meet our legal and audit obligations.

11. Changes to these Application Terms

11.1. We may update these Application Terms from time to time. The current version is always available in the Portal. Material changes apply to applications submitted after the change, and we will notify existing suppliers of them by email or in the Portal.
